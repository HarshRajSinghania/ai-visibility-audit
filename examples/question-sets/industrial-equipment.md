# Question set: Industrial equipment

Locale: US (English). Questions are written for a US market and use US English spelling and terminology. For other locales, translate and adjust product terminology, units, and regulatory references while keeping the intent structure.

Comparability: These questions are designed to be reusable across audits of the same brand. Keep the exact text when re-running so hit rates and rankings remain comparable. Record the locale and date of the audit with the results.

Evidence collection: For each question, capture the full AI answer, whether the brand is mentioned, the rank/position if mentioned, cited URLs, competitor names that appear, and third-party sources.
