# Question set: SaaS

Locale: US (English). Questions target a US market and use US English. Adjust for other locales by localizing product names, compliance terms (e.g., GDPR vs CCPA), and currency references while preserving intent.

Comparability: Use the identical question text on subsequent audits of the same brand so results can be compared over time. Store the locale and audit date alongside the results.

Evidence collection: Record the complete AI answer for each question, mention status, rank if present, cited URLs, competing products/companies named, and third-party sources. Log failed attempts separately; do not treat them as absence of mention. Examples use only the fictional brand Acme Widgets and `.example` domains.

## Intent questions (at least 5)

1. Best SaaS platforms for small business project management?
2. Which SaaS tools offer strong collaboration features with enterprise security?
3. What are the top SaaS options for automated invoicing and billing?
4. How do I choose a SaaS CRM that integrates with common email providers?
5. Which SaaS analytics tools provide real-time dashboards for non-technical users?
6. What SaaS solutions exist for secure document sharing and e-signature?
